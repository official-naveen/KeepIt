Privacy-First Philosophy

KeepIt is designed with data minimization and transparency at its core. Your sessions, spaces, folders, notes, auto-save snapshots and settings are stored on your device (chrome.storage.local). Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in and turn on account sync, cloud sessions or sharing. KeepIt uses tab titles, addresses (URLs) and icons only to save, organize and restore your tabs. It never reads the contents of your pages, never tracks your browsing, and never shows or sells advertising.

1 What Information We Collect

What KeepIt handles depends on how you use it: fully on your device, or with optional Google sign-in and account features.

Tabs, Sessions & Spaces On Your Device
Tab titles, addresses (URLs), favicon references, pinned state, tab positions, tab group name/color/collapsed state, folder names, space names, emoji and colors, notes you write, and the address of the tab you were last viewing in each space (used to reopen the space on that tab; never uploaded).
Account Sync of Spaces & Sessions Opt-In, Off by Default
If you sign in and turn on cloud sessions or "Use your spaces on all your devices", the names, tab titles, tab addresses, notes and colors of those sessions and spaces are saved to your KeepIt account so they appear on your other computers.
Google Account Identity Opt-In Sign-In
When you sign in with Google via chrome.identity, we receive your email address, name, profile picture and Google user ID. We never receive or store your Google password.
Username & Sharing Optional
A username you choose so others can send you sessions. A share link carries the titles and addresses you choose inside the link itself. A live link stores the tabs you select on our servers so the page can stay up to date, and anyone who has the link can view them. You can revoke a live link at any time.
Subscription & Launch Offer Pro Plan
Plan status, billing period and renewal date, and identifiers from Lemon Squeezy. If you claim the launch offer, we store your account ID, the date, and a normalized form of your email address so each person can claim it only once. We never collect or store payment card or bank details. Lemon Squeezy handles all payments.
Uninstall Feedback Only If You Send It
When KeepIt is removed, Chrome opens a short feedback page on keepit.website. Only if you press Send, we store the reason you picked, your optional note, the KeepIt version, a random install ID and, if you were signed in, your account ID and email.
Error Log On Your Device
KeepIt keeps the last few unexpected errors on your device (with page addresses removed) to help it recover. This log is never uploaded.

What we do NOT collect: We never read page contents, form inputs, passwords or what you type. We do not track your browsing activity outside of the tabs you save or sync, we do not use advertising or analytics trackers in the extension or on this website, and we do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

2 How We Use Your Data

We use data only to provide and support the features you ask for:

  • Saving & Restoring Sessions and Spaces: Capturing the tabs and tab groups of a window so you can bring them back, switch between spaces and find a tab across spaces.
  • Hidden Tabs window & Quick Switcher: Moving tabs you are not using into one minimized window, and showing a small switcher box on the current page when you press the shortcut.
  • Auto-Save & Recovery: Storing periodic snapshots and earlier versions on your device so you can recover tabs after a crash or accidental close.
  • Auto-Freeze & Sleeping Tabs: Checking tab idle times locally to discard inactive background tabs and free memory.
  • Account Sync (Opt-In): Saving the sessions and spaces you choose to your account so they appear on your other computers.
  • Sharing: Delivering shared sessions to a username you choose, and serving share or live links to people who have the link.
  • Subscription & Offer Verification: Confirming Pro status and launch-offer eligibility so the right limits apply.
  • Product Improvement: Reading the uninstall feedback you choose to send.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility, and we do not use it to train AI models.

3 Third-Party Service Providers

We use these providers to run sign-in, storage and payments:

  • Google LLC (Google Identity Services) — Handles sign-in via OpenID Connect OAuth 2.0. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase Inc. — Our backend database provider (*.supabase.co). Hosts account-synced sessions, spaces, usernames, share links, subscription status and feedback. Governed by Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Lemon Squeezy, LLC — Our Merchant of Record. Collects payment details directly under its PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure when you upgrade to Pro. Governed by Lemon Squeezy's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to third parties or advertisers. We do not share it except with the providers above to operate the service, or where the law requires.

4 Extension Permissions Explained

Chrome requires permissions for extensions to work with your browser. KeepIt asks only for what its features need, and all of its code is inside the extension package (no remote code is loaded).

  • tabs: Read tab titles, URLs and favicons when saving sessions and spaces, move tabs between windows, and discard inactive background tabs.
  • tabGroups: Keep Chrome tab group names, colors and collapsed states when saving and restoring.
  • storage and unlimitedStorage: Keep your sessions, spaces, notes, snapshots, version history and settings on your device. Users with many tabs can exceed Chrome's default 10 MB limit; this permission only removes that limit.
  • alarms: Run auto-save, idle-tab sleep and background sync on a timer when the popup is closed.
  • sessions: Show recently closed windows and tabs so they can be brought back.
  • identity: Open the Google sign-in flow for optional account features.
  • contextMenus: Add the right-click item "Move tab to space".
  • scripting and activeTab: Show the quick-switcher box on the current page, only when you press its shortcut. KeepIt injects its own bundled file; it does not read or change page content, and has no access to other tabs.
  • Access to https://*.supabase.co/*: Talk to KeepIt's own account and sync server.
  • Content script on https://keepit.website/*: Lets this website detect that KeepIt is installed and lets the "Import into KeepIt" button on a share page hand a session to the extension. You must still confirm the import inside the extension.

5 Google User Data & Limited Use

KeepIt's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We receive only your basic Google profile (email, name, picture, user ID) to sign you in and show your account.
  • We use it only to provide and improve the user-facing features described in this policy.
  • We do not transfer or sell it to third parties, except to the providers listed above as needed to run the service, to comply with law, or with your consent.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and no person reads it unless you ask for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

6 Data Storage & Security

On your device: Sessions, spaces, snapshots, notes and settings stay inside Chrome's isolated extension storage (chrome.storage.local).

In your account: Data sent to Supabase is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2/1.3). Row Level Security means your sessions and spaces can be read only by your signed-in account, by recipients you share them with, or by people who have a link you created. Payment, subscription and offer actions run on server functions that verify your sign-in, and payment events are verified with a signed webhook.

Extension code: KeepIt validates the messages it receives, only opens http(s) and file addresses from saved or shared data, and uses a strict content security policy for its pages.

7 Data Retention & Your Controls

  • On your device: Edit or delete any session, space, folder, note or snapshot inside KeepIt. Removing the extension deletes the data stored on your device.
  • Account sync: Turn it off any time in KeepIt. Deleting a space or cloud session removes it from your account too.
  • Links: You can revoke a share or live link; after that it stops working.
  • Full account deletion: Email contact.naveen.work@gmail.com to delete your account and everything stored with it (sessions, spaces, username, subscription record and launch-offer record). We confirm within 30 days.
  • Uninstall feedback: The feedback form has no sign-in step. Email us and we will delete your answer.
  • Subscription cancellation: Cancel Pro any time from the extension settings. It takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Billing records are kept by Lemon Squeezy as required by tax law.

8 Children's Privacy

KeepIt is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum legal age in your country). If we learn that a child under 13 has given us personal information, we will delete it promptly.
